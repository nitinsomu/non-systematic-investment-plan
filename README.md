# Non-Systematic Investment Plan (NSIP)

An alternative to regular SIP — analyze assets and get smart alerts on *when* and *how much* to invest based on market conditions.

## What it does

- Compares growth of assets (mutual funds, stocks, ETFs, etc.) under different investment strategies vs regular SIP
- Tracks market signals and triggers investment alerts instead of investing fixed amounts on fixed dates
- Configurable dashboard to set thresholds, asset watchlists, and notification preferences

## Approach

Rather than investing a fixed amount every month, NSIP monitors asset prices and signals (dips, RSI, moving averages, etc.) to suggest opportunistic entry points — investing more when conditions are favorable and less (or nothing) otherwise.
